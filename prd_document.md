# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Product: AptioGen (Next-Gen Assessment Platform)

### 1. Product Vision
AptioGen aims to become the definitive assessment platform for institutions by providing a seamless, secure, and data-driven testing environment. Our vision is to empower educators with high-integrity automation while offering students a fair and transparent testing experience.

### 2. Target Audience
- **Institution Administrators**: Deciding on standardizing assessments across campus.
- **Heads of Department (HODs)**: Managing department-specific student performance.
- **Students**: Taking exams with real-time feedback and results.
- **Super Admins**: Oversight of the platform's overall operations.

### 3. Core Features & Functionality

#### 3.1 Multi-Tiered Dashboards
- **Super Admin**: Global institution oversight, central question bank, system maintenance.
- **Inst-Admin**: Management of Departments, Batches, Events, and analytics.
- **HOD/Staff**: Departmental student monitoring and access control.

#### 3.2 Secure Quiz Engine
- **Event Wizard**: Simplified 4-step quiz launch process.
- **Randomization**: Server-side randomization of question order and option order.
- **Attempt Management**: Customizable attempt counts per student per event.

#### 3.3 Advanced Proctoring System
- **Environment Control**: Mandatory fullscreen mode, disabled copy-paste.
- **Tab Switching Detection**: Count and warn/submit on tab switch violations.
- **Webcam/Face Monitoring (Phased)**: Placeholder infrastructure for identity verification.

#### 3.4 Data & Analytics
- **Live Monitoring**: Real-time tracking of participants and their scores.
- **Score Visibility & Rankings**: Configurable ranking display (Top students, scores, or hidden).
- **Data Portability**: Full CSV exports and institution-wide performance reports.

### 4. User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Clarity over Complexity**: Use simple, intuitive wizards for event creation.
- **Mobile First**: Fully responsive student assessment interface.
- **Instant Gratification**: Automated grading and immediate score transparency (where enabled).
- **Dark/Light Mode**: User-selectable themes for comfortable prolonged usage.

### 5. Success Metrics
- **Ease of Adoption**: Institution staff can launch their first assessment in < 10 minutes.
- **Integrity**: Zero reported cases of undetected cheating during proctored exams.
- **Technical Stability**: Support for 1,000+ total registered users with 0% database corruption.
- **Admin Satisfaction**: High NPS score from institution administrators for the analytics module.

### 6. Roadmap & Future Scope
- **AI-Driven Analytics**: Predictive modeling for student performance.
- **Automated Certificate Generation**: PDF certificates with verifiable QR codes.
- **Native Mobile Apps**: Dedicated Android/iOS apps for push-notified assessments.
- **CMS Integration**: Direct API connections with common LMS platforms.
